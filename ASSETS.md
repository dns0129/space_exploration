## Other celestial models

The Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune use procedural materials implemented in `src/planet-models.ts`. Noise, crater fields, latitude bands, storm shapes, polar caps, ring density and solar surface patterns are computed by shaders. Saturn's ring geometry and shadows, solar prominence curves, atmospheric halos and the star field are generated locally. No additional third-party planetary images are downloaded or embedded for these models.

These are artistic representations of recognizable planetary features, not measured maps, current weather data or physically complete astronomical simulations. Observation mode normalizes each body separately; flight mode places all nine bodies in one world with compressed distances and display radii.

The spacecraft hull, wings, cockpit, engine exhaust and flight HUD are generated by the application. They use no third-party spacecraft imagery or models.
